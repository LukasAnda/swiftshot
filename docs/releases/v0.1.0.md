First release.

Swiftshot turns your SwiftUI and UIKit `#Preview`s into App Store screenshots without booting a simulator.

- Every device, iOS version, language, appearance and text size, from one command
- Pixel identical to a booted simulator, checked in CI
- iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo and Apple Watch
- Sheets, alerts, partial views and full-length (scrolling) screenshots
- Output in fastlane/deliver layout

On a free GitHub runner: 73 screenshots in under a minute, where fastlane needs about 54 minutes for 45.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with Xcode 26.6 or 27.x.
