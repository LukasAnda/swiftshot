- Flutter: widget `@Preview`s (Flutter 3.35+) for iPhones, iPads and Android devices, on a Mac or Linux. Run `swiftshot` in the folder with `pubspec.yaml`.
- Preview fields `name`, `size`, `textScaleFactor`, `wrapper`, `theme`, `brightness` and `localizations`; `MultiPreview`, custom subclasses and `transform()`. Found in `lib/` without building.
- Headless: `flutter test` with Impeller, one process per language, with system bars over full screens. Fonts for Arabic, Hebrew, CJK, Thai and Devanagari from the system.
- Exact iOS on a Mac with Xcode: iPhones and iPads render on Flutter's iOS engine, pixel identical to a booted simulator, with the real status bar. `--flutter-ios headless` to skip it.
- The same options as SwiftUI and Compose: `screens`, `font_scales`, `full_length`, device frames (`--accept-bezel-license` for iPhones) and the fastlane layout. Layout overflows are warnings.
- `--flutter-root` / `flutter_root`, else `FLUTTER_ROOT`, the project's fvm version or `flutter` on `PATH`.
- `swiftshot init` takes the languages from the gen-l10n ARB files; `swiftshot devices` lists iPhones and iPads too.
- `Example/FlutterDemo`: 204 screenshots (iPhone and Pixel, five languages, light and dark) in about 11 s on an Apple silicon Mac.
